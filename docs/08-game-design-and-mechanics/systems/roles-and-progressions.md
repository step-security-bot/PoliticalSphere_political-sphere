## Overview

Political Sphere offers diverse starting positions and merit-based progression paths across parliamentary, judicial, and media institutions. Players advance through reputation and performance, not time or payment, and can lose positions through elections or poor performance.

---

## Core Principles

- **Diverse Entry Points:** Players choose from multiple starting roles
- **Merit-Based Advancement:** Progression based solely on reputation and achievement
- **No Pay-to-Win:** Zero monetary shortcuts; all advancement earned
- **Dynamic Positions:** Roles can be gained and lost based on performance and elections
- **Player Priority:** Human players preferred for leadership roles over AI NPCs
- **Cross-Institution Mobility:** Limited movement between branches (e.g., MP to Judge requires special circumstances)

## Starting Roles

### Parliamentary Branch

#### Member of Parliament (MP)

**Description:** Elected representative in the legislature

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Speak in debates
- Vote on legislation
- Propose bills (with limitations)
- Join committees
- Ask questions of ministers

**Starting Resources:**
- Basic office allocation
- Small staff budget
- Limited influence points

**Starting Reputation:** Neutral (0)

**Requirements:** None (default starting role)

---

#### Backbench MP (Variant)

**Description:** Non-ministerial MP focused on constituency and select committees

**Special Focus:**
- Committee work emphasis
- Private members' bills
- Constituency advocacy
- Cross-party collaboration

---

### Judicial Branch

#### Junior Judge

**Description:** Lower court judge handling procedural and minor matters

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Rule on procedural challenges
- Arbitrate constitutional questions (minor)
- Interpret legislation
- Judicial review of administrative actions

**Starting Resources:**
- Chambers allocation
- Judicial clerk support
- Legal research access

**Starting Reputation:** Neutral (0)

**Requirements:**
- Character creation selection
- Complete judicial tutorial
- Demonstrate understanding of constitutional principles

**Restrictions:**
- Cannot hold parliamentary office
- Cannot engage in political campaigning
- Limited public commentary

---

### Media Branch

#### Junior Journalist

**Description:** Reporter covering political events and generating public opinion

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Publish articles (subject to editorial review)
- Interview politicians
- Attend press conferences
- Access parliamentary press gallery

**Starting Resources:**
- Basic newsroom access
- Limited publication slots per day
- Modest readership reach

**Starting Reputation:** Neutral (0)

**Requirements:**
- Character creation selection
- Complete media ethics tutorial

**Special Mechanics:**
- Articles affect public opinion and politician reputations
- Accuracy and fairness tracked (impacts own reputation)
- Can investigate and break stories

---

#### Editor (Advanced Media Start)

**Description:** Editorial position with greater influence and responsibilities

**Additional Capabilities:**
- Editorial control over publication tone
- Hire/fire reporters (AI and potentially junior players)
- Set publication agenda
- Greater reach and influence

**Requirements:**
- Special character creation option
- Higher starting reputation requirement
- Demonstrate media literacy in tutorial

---

### Executive Branch

#### Junior Civil Servant

**Description:** Administrative official supporting government operations

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Draft policy papers
- Provide ministerial briefings
- Coordinate departmental operations
- Implement approved policies

**Starting Resources:**
- Department access
- Administrative authority
- Policy drafting tools

**Restrictions:**
- Non-political role (serve any government)
- Cannot publicly advocate policy positions
- Cannot stand for election while serving

**Requirements:** Character creation selection

---

### Other Institutional Roles

#### Think Tank Researcher

**Description:** Policy analyst producing research and recommendations

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Publish research papers
- Provide expert testimony to committees
- Brief politicians and media
- Organize policy events

**Special Mechanics:**
- Research quality affects political discourse
- Can influence policy direction without direct power
- Building credibility unlocks consultancy opportunities

---

#### Lobbyist/Advocate

**Description:** Represents interest groups and causes

**Starting Capabilities:**
- Meet with MPs and ministers
- Submit evidence to committees
- Organize campaigns
- Build coalitions

**Restrictions:**
- Must register and disclose clients
- Transparency requirements
- Ethical boundaries enforced

## Progression Paths

### Parliamentary Progression

**Backbench MP** → **Committee Chair** → **Whip** → **Minister** → **Secretary of State** → **Prime Minister**

Alternative paths:
- **Speaker:** Elected by house, requires cross-party respect
- **Opposition Leader:** Lead opposing party
- **Party Leader:** Lead own party (if created or elected)

---

### Judicial Progression

**Junior Judge** → **Senior Judge** → **Appeal Court Judge** → **Supreme Court Justice** → **Chief Justice**

**Advancement Criteria:**
- Quality of judgments
- Legal reasoning reputation
- Impartiality maintenance
- Scholarly contributions

**Special Notes:**
- Very limited positions at senior levels
- Appointment process involves multi-branch approval
- Lifetime or long-term appointments

---

### Media Progression

**Junior Journalist** → **Senior Reporter** → **Columnist** → **Editor** → **Chief Editor** → **Media Mogul**

Alternative path:
- **Broadcaster:** Television/radio personality
- **Investigative Journalist:** Specialist in exposés
- **Political Commentator:** Opinion and analysis focus

**Advancement Criteria:**
- Story quality and impact
- Readership/viewership
- Journalistic integrity
- Breaking significant stories

---

### Cross-Branch Movement

**Restrictions:**
- Judges cannot return to politics after appointment
- MPs must resign to become judges (rare)
- Media professionals can enter politics (must disclose)
- Civil servants must resign before standing for election

## Reputation System

### Reputation Sources

**Positive Reputation:**
- Quality debate contributions
- Successful legislation
- Keeping promises
- Cross-party collaboration
- Constituent service (MPs)
- Fair reporting (Media)
- Sound judgments (Judges)
- Ethical conduct

**Negative Reputation:**
- Procedural violations
- Breaking promises
- Partisan attacks
- Unparliamentary language
- Corruption or conflicts of interest
- Inaccurate reporting (Media)
- Bias (Judges)

### Reputation Effects

**High Reputation Benefits:**
- Greater influence in debates
- Media attention
- Election advantages (MPs)
- Appointment opportunities
- Resource allocation increases
- Cross-party respect

**Low Reputation Consequences:**
- Reduced speaking time
- Media criticism
- Electoral vulnerability (MPs)
- Party discipline issues
- Resource constraints
- Removal from positions

## Role-Specific Resources

### MPs

- **Office Budget:** Staff, research, constituency work
- **Influence Points:** Spend on amendments, motions, private bills
- **Time Allocation:** Balance chamber work, committees, constituency
- **Campaign Funds:** Electoral purposes (if in party)

### Judges

- **Chambers Budget:** Clerks, research, facilities
- **Case Load:** Number and complexity of cases
- **Judicial Independence:** Protected from political pressure

### Media

- **Publication Slots:** Number of stories per period
- **Investigation Budget:** Deep-dive investigative work
- **Reach:** Audience size and influence
- **Editorial Independence:** Freedom from owner interference

### Civil Servants

- **Departmental Budget:** Implementation resources
- **Policy Capacity:** Number of policy initiatives supported
- **Administrative Authority:** Decision-making scope

## Advancement Mechanics

### Merit-Based System

**No Automatic Advancement:**
- Time served does not guarantee promotion
- All progression tied to achievement and reputation
- Performance metrics tailored to role type

**Transparent Criteria:**
- Clear benchmarks for each progression step
- Public voting records (MPs)
- Published judgments (Judges)
- Article archive (Media)

**Competitive Selection:**
- Multiple players may compete for limited positions
- Selection processes appropriate to role (elections, appointments, editorial boards)
- AI NPCs can fill gaps but human players prioritized

---

### Losing Positions

**Electoral Loss (MPs):**
- Fail to win re-election
- Lose party confidence vote
- Constituency boundary changes

**Removal from Office:**
- Vote of no confidence (Prime Minister, Ministers)
- Impeachment (severe misconduct)
- Party expulsion (lose whip)

**Judicial Removal:**
- Misconduct tribunal
- Violation of judicial oath
- Demonstrated bias

**Media Termination:**
- Accuracy violations
- Ethical breaches
- Readership collapse
- Editorial disagreements

**Recovery Paths:**
- Re-contest elections
- Rebuild reputation
- Start in different role
- Seek alternative advancement

---

## AI NPC Role Distribution

**Parliamentary Seats:**
- AI NPCs fill vacant parliamentary seats
- Vote on legislation as members
- Can hold ministerial positions (human players preferred)
- Can be Prime Minister if no suitable human player

**Judicial Positions:**
- AI judges handle routine cases
- Senior positions prioritized for human players
- AI provides consistency and availability

**Media Landscape:**
- AI journalists create baseline news coverage
- AI editors run smaller publications
- Human players create premium/investigative content

**Dynamic Adjustment:**
- AI presence scales with player population
- As player base grows, AI roles decrease
- AI maintains world credibility even with low player counts

## Unlock System

### Abilities and Powers

**As roles advance, unlock:**

**MPs:**
- Priority speaking slots
- Amendment powers
- Bill sponsorship (expanded)
- Committee chair positions
- Ministerial powers (executive functions)

**Judges:**
- Constitutional review authority
- Precedent-setting cases
- Senior court assignments
- Judicial training/mentorship

**Media:**
- Exclusive interview access
- Editorial control
- Investigative resources
- Broadcasting opportunities
- Opinion column prominence

### Restrictions and Responsibilities

**Higher roles carry:**
- Greater accountability
- Ethical obligations
- Conflict of interest rules
- Public scrutiny
- Resource management duties

## Integration with Other Systems

### Party System

- MPs benefit significantly from party membership (resources, support, coordination)
- Independent MPs have harder path but more flexibility
- Media and judges cannot be party members
- Civil servants serve neutrally

### Elections

- MPs face regular elections
- Performance affects re-election chances
- Media coverage influences electoral outcomes
- Judicial appointments involve confirmation processes

### Economy

- All roles have budgets and resource constraints
- Financial management affects effectiveness
- Corruption/misuse has severe consequences
